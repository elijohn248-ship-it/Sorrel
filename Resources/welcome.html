Welcome to Drift

Spaces, a sidebar that gets out of the way, and bangs — on Safari’s engine.

Start here

⌘TNew tab. Type a search, an address, a !bang, an open tab’s name, or a command.
⌘LEdit the current tab’s address, right in the sidebar’s address box. Clicking the box does the same.
⌘SHide or show the sidebar. While hidden, move the cursor to the left edge to peek at it.
⌘DPin or unpin the tab in this space.
⌘⇧DAdd the tab to Favorites, the tiles that stay put in every space.
⌘⇧CCopy the page’s link. Hovering the address box shows a copy button too.
⌘W ⌘⇧TClose a tab, reopen the last one closed.

Spaces and folders

⌥⌘← ⌥⌘→Previous and next space. A two-finger swipe on the sidebar does the same.
⌃1 … ⌃9Jump to a space.
⌘1 … ⌘9Jump to a favorite or pinned tab.
⌃⌘NNew folder. Drag pinned tabs and other folders into it.
⌘K ⌘TCustomize this space: name, icon, colors and gradient.

Right-click any empty part of the sidebar and choose Customize Space. Right-click a space’s icon at the bottom to give it its own separate logins or delete it.

Previews and mini windows

Click a linkA link to another site opens as a preview over the page, so you keep your place. Search results open normally. Shift-click previews any link.
⌘O EscTurn the preview into a full tab, or close it. The buttons at its top right do the same.
⌥⌘NOpen a mini window. Once Drift is your default browser, links from other apps open in one too.
⌘OAlso turns a mini window into a full tab, as does “Open in …” at its top right.

Privacy

Ads and trackersuBlock Origin Lite is built in and already on. Click the puzzle piece at the bottom left of the sidebar to open it, change how strict it is on a site, or remove an element from a page.
ExtensionsDrift runs Safari-style extensions. The ones already on your Mac are listed in Settings ▸ Extensions, switched off until you turn them on.
⌘⇧NNew private window. It keeps no history, cookies or logins.
Old tabsUnpinned tabs you haven’t used for a day are archived. Find them under Tabs ▸ Archived Tabs.
MemoryTabs left alone for two hours go to sleep and reload where you left them. Change either in Settings ▸ Tabs.

Bangs

Bangs are handled on your Mac. !w kidney goes straight to Wikipedia without passing through a search engine.

!pm !up !ambPubMed, UpToDate, AMBOSS
!rad !mdcRadiopaedia, MDCalc
!w !yt !g !ghWikipedia, YouTube, Google, GitHub — and about 13,000 more

Add your own in bangs.json.

Make it yours

Press ⌘, for Settings: search engine, privacy, tabs, how the sidebar reveals itself, and every keyboard shortcut. Double-click a command there to record a new shortcut for it.

Everything is also plain files in the settings folder (Drift ▸ Settings Folder…). keybindings.json can hold two-step shortcuts like "cmd+k cmd+s", and ⌘K ⌘R reloads the files without restarting.
